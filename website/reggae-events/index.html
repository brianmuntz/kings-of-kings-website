Rub-A-Dub Live – Dinner & Dance | November 8, 2025 | Toronto

Thriller Music in association with Kings of Kings International proudly presents Rub-A-Dub Live , bringing the legendary Rub-A-Dub Thursdays from Jamaica to Canada for the first time ever!

Join an all-star lineup of reggae icons including Icho Candy , Errol Dunkley , Professor Nuts , Original Thriller , Shalom , and Iley Dread for a night of authentic roots reggae, dancehall, and foundation vibes.

The celebration continues with powerful performances by Miztriz , Majah Baines , Mel C , Genuine Affection , Wayne Passion , and Fada Price — plus special guests and surprise appearances throughout the evening.

Icho Candy Live in Toronto – November 7, 2025

For the first time in Canada , legendary Rub-A-Dub Thursday singer Icho Candy takes the stage in Toronto for an unforgettable reggae night presented by Thriller Music alongside Kings of Kings International .

Joining him will be reggae greats Errol Dunkley , Original Thriller , Donna Makeda , Iley Dread , Mel C. , and Kalico , with MC Delroy G (from Flow FM) and DJ Wiz (from 105.5 FM) keeping the vibes high. Expect surprise international guest appearances and authentic roots, lovers rock, and dancehall energy all night.

🎶 Music by: Kings of Kings Sounds & Routzman Sounds
