THE INTERNATIONAL STAGE

Capleton. Cocoa Tea. New York.

The Reign of Fire Tour flyer pairs Capleton with special guest Cocoa Tea at B.B. King Blues Club & Grill in New York. A Kings of Kings crest appears on the original artwork.

September 14 · year not printed on this flyer

FROM THE SUPPLIED ARCHIVE / 2011

Western Consciousness

The April 30, 2011 poster lists Beenie Man, Bounty Killer, I-Octane, Lutan Fyah, Romain Virgo, Tanya Stephens, Freddie McGregor, Turbulence and Iley Dread among its advertised lineup. The promoter is credited on the artwork as King of Kings Promotions.

Paradise Park, Westmoreland, Jamaica · April 30, 2011

RECORDS MEET THE STAGE

Lover’s Holiday, live in Toronto

A record-release showcase at the Annex WreckRoom brings together Colin Levy, Len Hammond, Geoffrey Star, Chrisinti and Nana McLean. The original flyer connects the record to a live lovers-rock experience.

December 19 · original release-showcase artwork
